Furniture

Case goods come first: chests, beds, desks and tables in island woods, cane and carved detail. The upholstered chairs and ottomans follow, and the volume closes with the eight custom fabrics, shown full frame.

Volume Two

Decor

Artwork, Lighting and Rugs

32 pages27 pieces3 sectionsPDF 19.3 MB

Thirteen works of art, nine pieces of lighting and five rugs, each on its own page. The artwork runs from Joseph Dwight Strong and his Diamond Head watercolour of 1886 to Hokusai, Jakuchū and Gauguin, and every piece is shown with a close study of its frame and matting corner. The lamps, chandelier and pendants follow, then the rugs, in wool and classic pattern.
